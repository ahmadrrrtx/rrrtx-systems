Website Launch & Conversion Checklist

45-Point Engineering & CRO Blueprint

A rigorous pre-launch guide created by RRRTX SYSTEMS engineers to guarantee sub-1s load times, perfect clarity, bulletproof credibility, and high conversion capture.

1. Performance & Technical SEO 10 Points

  • Sub-1s Mobile Render Time Check

    Verify your page loading scores on PageSpeed Insights. Eliminate render-blocking JS bundles.

  • Edge Cache Server Routing

    Deploy static pages on edge delivery servers (Vercel, Cloudflare) for localized low-latency loads.

  • Oversized Media Asset Compression

    Compress hero visuals into modern format codecs (.webp or .avif) to save network overhead.

2. Above-the-Fold (ATF) Clarity 8 Points

  • 3-Second Value Proposition Rule

    Ask an outside reviewer what you offer. If they don't grasp it within 3s, simplify your headlines.

  • High-Contrast Call-to-Action (CTA)

    Ensure your primary booking CTA stands out cleanly against abstract backdrops using neon accents.

3. Credibility & Trust Architecture 12 Points

  • Pre-built Integration scrolling marquee

    Assemble a moving ribbon of platforms your systems leverage (Stripe, WhatsApp, Make, Turso).

  • Real Founders Proof quotes

    Install rotating quote slider panels showcasing client faces rather than generic review text.
